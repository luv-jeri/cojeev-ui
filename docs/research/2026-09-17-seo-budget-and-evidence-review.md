# 000h SEO budget and evidence review

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-17. **Budget assumption:** $10–20 total initial cash, not permission for an ongoing subscription. Reviewed the 2026-09-16 research memo against current primary sources. No accounts, purchases, installations, or production changes were made.

## Decision

Build the technical and content foundations with the free stack first. Keep OpenSEO optional. A single hosted month is the plausible paid experiment within this budget; paid self-hosted DataForSEO is outside it. Buying data does not buy rankings.

## Suggested spend

- **Start: $0.** Establish Search Console/Bing access, useful page content, crawl checks, and baseline reporting. Use existing hosting and agent subscriptions; incremental fees from those services are outside this estimate and must stay within their current allowances.
- **Optional later: one $10 listed-price hosted month.** Buy only when the domain is verified, research questions and target pages are ready, and checkout total fits the cap. Reserve the remaining budget for applicable charges; leave it unspent otherwise. With a strict $10 total cap, stay free if checkout exceeds it.
- **No additional subscription, hosting, data deposit, paid backlink, ad campaign, or paid AI monitoring package.** Continue free reporting after the pilot.

Illustrative pilot usage: 40 keyword searches, 6 backlink overviews, weekly tracking of 20 keywords, and at most one ChatGPT brand check. The public estimator suggests about $0.05, $0.08, and $1.09 respectively for keyword search, backlink overview, and brand check; its default rank example is about $0.0025/check. These inputs imply roughly **$3.77 usage**, leaving headroom within the included allowance. This is arithmetic planning, not a quote: result limits, workflow requests, countries, and settings change consumption. [Estimator](https://openseo.so/pricing)

Use cached/exported results, one target country initially, weekly tracking, and a recorded $5 internal usage stop for the first research batch. Produce a prioritized keyword-to-page map and competitor opportunity list. Skip expensive repeated brand checks and paid full-site crawling that the local checks can cover. Evaluate a second month only if the first changed useful publishing decisions; it is not included in this initial plan.

## Review of Google claims

**The previous `llms.txt` statement is verified.** Google's current guide explicitly says it ignores these files for Google Search visibility and rankings. It permits maintaining them for other systems. Keep a generated file as optional developer documentation convenience; do not represent it as a Google ranking investment. [Google guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

**The dedicated generative-AI report claim is also verified.** Google's announcement is dated June 3, 2026 and updated to state worldwide rollout on August 31. [Announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports)

**Qualify availability and metrics:** the help page documents impressions by page, country, device, and date for AI Overviews/AI Mode, and says insufficient impressions or rollout can explain an absent report. It does not establish dedicated AI clicks, CTR, ranking positions, or prompt/query reporting. Check the actual property; record unavailable separately from zero. The help page also points to a Search generative-AI inclusion control, which belongs in the baseline check. [Report documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139)

## Changes recommended for the overall plan

1. Remove any mandatory paid OpenSEO/DataForSEO dependency from the baseline checkpoint.
2. Keep first-party measurement and local technical audits sufficient to complete the initial delivery.
3. Make the hosted pilot a separate optional task with a final checkout total, one billing period, export deliverables, and renewal cancellation evidence.
4. Preserve the Google findings with the metric and property-availability qualifications above.
5. Treat all catalogue counts and competitor snapshots in the older memo as dated evidence until reconciled with the current production catalogue.
