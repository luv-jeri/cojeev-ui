# Expansion evidence

The four inventories enumerate canonical public catalogue entries at pinned source revisions. They distinguish discovery from individual source inspection, rendered samples and completed Cojeev work. `coverage.json` is the cross-source adaptation ledger; regenerate inventory facts with `node scripts/sync-expansion-ledger.mjs` while preserving recorded implementation progress.

Upstream source, page and registry captures in each `receipts/` directory retain their original licenses. They are local research evidence, ignored by Git and excluded from Cojeev registry payloads. They are not relicensed under Cojeev's MIT license. The public source URLs and hashes recorded in inventories establish provenance without distributing those captures.

All first-batch production additions are independently authored Cojeev code. Renaming or recoloring restricted third-party source is not our implementation strategy. Read the per-source research files for exact reuse boundaries and remaining work. In particular, the first two shader backgrounds establish shared rendering infrastructure; they do not close any Canvas HTML-refraction or particle-object row.
